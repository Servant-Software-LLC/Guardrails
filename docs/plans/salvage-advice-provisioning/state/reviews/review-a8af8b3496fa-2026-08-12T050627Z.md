## The central correction

The original fix applied two changes: **(A)** gave wave-03/02 ownership of the pre-existing golden
`ClaudePromptRunnerArgsTests.cs` via `writeScope`, and **(B)** narrowed the wave-3 exit gate filter from
`~ToolGrant|~ClaudePromptRunner` to `~ToolGrantInjection`.

**(A) and (B) are complements that were applied as alternatives.** (A) grants the ownership; (B) removes
the obligation. Together they left **nothing in wave 3 verifying the re-baseline at all**. The claim that
wave 4's full-suite gate would catch it was **wrong**, on two counts:

- `ClaudePromptRunnerArgsTests.cs` is in **no** wave-4 `writeScope`, so a bad re-baseline reappears as an
  *unfixable* wave-4 halt one wave later, after four more tasks have spent their budget.
- A **deleted** test cannot fail a full-suite gate. Deletion ships silently.

(A) makes a failure fixable by the task that caused it. That is what this addendum does.
