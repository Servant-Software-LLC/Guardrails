## What changed after the stamp, and why

A cross-cutting compatibility question — *"does this arc collide with #223 (local inference)?"* — turned
up one field-level forward-compatibility defect that had survived the review pass.

**Task 01 (`TelemetryRow`): `kind` was specified as a field with no stated type.** An implementer who
reached for `PromptRunnerKind` would have produced a corpus that rejects, or silently drops, the first
row from a provider registered after this code was written — which is exactly the provider the corpus
exists to evaluate (`openai-compat` arrives with #223, and local inference is the reason for the arc
this plan belongs to). The corpus is an **archive**: its job is to record what the journal said, not to
have an opinion about it. `JournalTierSpend` already set that precedent one level up, reporting an
unrecognized rung rather than discarding it.

1. The prompt now requires `kind`, `model`, `tier`, `tierSource` and `runner` to be recorded as
   **verbatim strings, never a re-validated enum**, with the reasoning and the `JournalTierSpend`
   precedent named.

Point 2 is load-bearing and was not optional: point 1 alone would have been a prose requirement with no
guardrail behind it, which is the #221 prose-only-prohibition shape the review pass had just finished
checking for. Adding the instruction without the census row would have introduced the exact finding
class the pass exists to catch.
